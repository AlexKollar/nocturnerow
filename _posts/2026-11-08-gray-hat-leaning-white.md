The founding stance of Nocturne Row, written the night the group got its name.

The capabilities stay real and sharp. There is no virtue in a dull tool, and no honesty in pretending a security group builds harmless things. The lean toward white is not in what the tools *can’t* do — it’s in the defaults: consent before access, authorization before action, and an audit trail that lets anyone check the work afterwards.

Sharp and accountable. Everything in the open, including this.

*(The group’s motto has since become "Walk with the shadows." This essay keeps the title it was founded under.)*
