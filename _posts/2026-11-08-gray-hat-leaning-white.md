Say it plain: we're gray hat, leaning white. We know every trick the night shift runs — because we've run them — and we spend that knowledge keeping the net safe instead of fleecing it.

And we leave the light on. Anyone can check the work, any time they like. That's the lean.

Open source. Local first. Proven in the light. Not a slogan — a habit, kept nightly.

Walk with shadows.

— The Row
