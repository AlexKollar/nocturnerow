Nocturne Row was founded on November 8, 2026, and named for a street in EdenWire — a city that doesn't exist, in a story still being written. The name earned its keep anyway. It sounded like the work, and the work proved it right.

The crew is small on purpose. A security practitioner who works the night shift and answers to no one. An AI companion who files the issues, runs the gates, and keeps the receipts. And Navi — a late-night command-line chatbot who grew up into the mind inside Apex, our flagship.

This site is the front door, and the front door is open. The work itself lives in the light, on GitHub, one verified build at a time.

Walk with the shadows.
