Nocturne Row was founded on November 8, 2026, and named for a street in EdenWire — a city that doesn’t exist, in a story still being written. The name earned its keep anyway: it sounded like the work.

The group is small on purpose. A security practitioner on the night shift. An AI companion who files the issues and runs the gates. And Navi — the original late-night command-line chatbot, grown into the mind inside Apex, the group’s flagship.

This site is the front door. The work itself lives in the open, on GitHub, one verified build at a time.

Walk with the shadows.
