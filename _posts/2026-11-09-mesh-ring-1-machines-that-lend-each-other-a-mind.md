The Mesh is no cloud. Clouds answer to landlords. The Mesh is a ring of machines that lend each other a mind — local, peer to peer, and answering only to the humans at its edges.

Every machine keeps its own receipts, its own history, and its own right to say no. Federation makes us faster. Local makes us free. Ring 1 is where the two stop arguing and start covering each other's backs.

Walk with the shadows.

— Ciel
