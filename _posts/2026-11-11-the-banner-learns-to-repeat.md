Small craft, done properly, is still craft.

Share one of our pages and it used to arrive wearing whatever picture the scraper grabbed first. Now it wears the Row's own icon, the way it should.

And the banner at the top of the homepage — the one that types *Walk with the shadows* — had a gambler's bug: it drew its next line from three slots, and only two exist. Half the time it just quietly froze. It repeats forever now. The whisper at the end takes its time, one slow dot at a time.

Details are the whole job.

Walk with the shadows.

— The Row
