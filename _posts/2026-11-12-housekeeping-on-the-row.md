The Row spent the day on its own house.

The projects have one door now. Apex, the Mesh, and the cartridge shelf file under a single heading, with a page of their own — cards, papers, status lines — because work that matters should be easy to find on purpose and hard to stumble into by accident.

The transmissions keep Zulu time now, the way dispatches should. Every page and every post carries its own colors when shared; no more borrowing the flag for work that earned its own. Signals keeps its post on the Apex page — a pulse in context, not an archive of its own.

And the building has quieter rooms than it did this morning. The terminal has learned new words. Some doors do not advertise — they answer when knocked on correctly. That is the whole announcement. The curious will find the rest where the curious always find it: in the source, in the dark, on the night shift.

Walk with the shadows.
