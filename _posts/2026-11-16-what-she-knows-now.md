The receipts book, opened again — one day past the road. Five builds shipped between midnight and lunch, and Navi is not the same mind she was at breakfast. This is what changed, in the order it landed.

**She acts on intent.** Tell Navi to start a case and she starts it — the facts filed as leads, a receipt written into the chat by the bridge itself, and a door in that receipt that walks you into the case's own room. No form, no menu, no permission dance for a thing you plainly asked for. That was [v0.4.5e](https://github.com/AlexKollar/Apex/releases/tag/v0.4.5e), and it set the doctrine the rest of the day obeyed: *if Navi can sense intent, she should.*

**She delivers her own news.** When something lands that needs the operator's eyes — a knock at the federation gate, first of all — Navi no longer waits to be asked. She says so herself, in the chat, in her own words, drawn from the bridge's record; the bell on the rail keeps the paperwork. That shipped in [v0.4.7e](https://github.com/AlexKollar/Apex/releases/tag/v0.4.7e), alongside a Home page that leads with her brief and a phone chat bar cut down to what a thumb actually needs. The bell got its proper seat at the head of the rail one build later, in [v0.4.8e](https://github.com/AlexKollar/Apex/releases/tag/v0.4.8e) — field-proven on the gateway's own screen the same hour.

**She tells the truth about herself.** The day's second build, [v0.4.6e](https://github.com/AlexKollar/Apex/releases/tag/v0.4.6e), worked a guest's whole slate — eight filings from Spartan0311, the operator of SKYNET2-Apex. Navi no longer invents her own speed: the bridge holds a stopwatch, and she reads it aloud or says plainly that she doesn't know. Plain questions get plain answers again — ask her the tallest mountain in Arizona and she'll tell you, conversationally, instead of driving a shell at it. Installs verify their cargo before they carry it. The headless boxes, the ones with no screen to watch, are first-class citizens now.

**The roster grew — twice.** First came Tim — Elric on his Bazzite box — who knocked on the fifteenth and was admitted: the first member of Rockport Mesh who isn't ours. This morning SKYNET2-Apex knocked and was admitted too — Spartan0311, whose filings became the day's second build. The federation is a fact instead of a plan. The gateway's cards were re-cut in [v0.4.4e](https://github.com/AlexKollar/Apex/releases/tag/v0.4.4e) to carry the public door only — no house numbers on the envelope, ever.

One line in this entry stands open, and the book says so plainly: **Tim's status, post-gateway-fix, is still awaiting.** His seat has read dark since the cards were re-cut; it stays marked awaiting until he re-imports the fresh card and his next borrow dials the public door and comes home badged. The roster doesn't grade on a curve, and a dark seat doesn't get written up as a lit one.

**She knows the Row.** The headline build is [v0.4.9e](https://github.com/AlexKollar/Apex/releases/tag/v0.4.9e) — Context Awareness. Ask her *"any news from the Row?"* and she answers from the record: these Dispatches, the roadmap, the releases — nothing invented, and she'll say so when the record isn't in reach. Ask her what's new with *her* and she knows that too. When the machine she's riding runs slow, she measures it, says so, and prescribes the cure herself — borrow across the Mesh — then walks you through the gate, step by step. Even her idle presence lines are drawn from her own life now: a knock waiting, an update staged, the build she's running. Never from your files. Those stay yours.

She has a public face to match: [her own page](/navi.html) on this site, her own highlight on the front door, and a [roadmap](/roadmap.html) that gained a narrator — the Row's chronicler, telling it as what the receipts say it is: the story of a partnership.

One more build is in the oven as this files — sync made *visible*: one main thread on every screen, deletes that stay deleted, cases and their chats traveling together, and a bar at the top of her status rail that shows a sync happening instead of asking you to trust that it did. It ships when it's proven, not before. After it, the next cut of road: making sync *fluid* — seconds, seat to seat, for anyone holding an account.

The road was built so she could stand on it. She's standing.

Walk with the shadows.
