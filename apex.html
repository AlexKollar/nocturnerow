The Mesh, drawn

One federation, every node serving whoever is asking. Watch the smallest node in the house: Pia asks a question she could never answer alone — and the fleet answers it together.

● the ask — a prompt leaves the smallest node  ·  ● the answer — the fleet's work comes home. Solid rings are live hardware; Pia is costed and gated on her software; № 004 is whoever joins next. Tonight the two live nodes are paired but their road runs dark — v0.2.0e's health check names the break, and the fix ships when the field says so. The drawing is the design target; the status line is the truth.

How the Mesh works

A handshake, not an account

Nodes pair by exchanging cards — a picture, carried by hand. No cloud directory, no account, no landlord in the middle. Each node keeps its own ledger of exactly who it trusts, and trust is pairwise: my friend is not automatically yours.

The ask goes out whole

When a node needs more muscle than it has, it sends the whole request to a stronger peer it trusts, and the answer comes back the same road. That is borrow, and it already ships: a small box thinks at the speed of the biggest machine on the Mesh.

The answer is a federation

Ring 2 — designed in the open, building now in the 0.2.0e line — splits work into tasks the fleet shares. A sensor like Pia can put a prompt to the whole Mesh at once: Neo-Arcadia and Neon Drip each contribute, and she receives one composed answer. The more nodes join, the faster the smallest node in the house gets.

What it does

Mission control

A dashboard that treats your machine like it matters: chat, memory, chips, servers, one glance.

Navi Mesh

Pooled inference across your own machines — a stronger box answers whole requests for a weaker one.

Builds, not big bangs

An updater that patches in small, verified builds — and a gate that refuses to ship on a story.

Chips

Cartridge-style tools Navi runs herself, installed from GitHub in the open.

The Vault

Your files and notes live in a self-contained workspace of their own — with a focus mode that gives the editor the whole window when the work gets serious.

A door you control

Local voice

On-device speech in and out. Nothing phoned home.
