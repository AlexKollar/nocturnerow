TRANSMISSION // UNLISTED — AN OLD SAINTSSEC TRADITION, CARRIED TO THE ROW

if you're going to try, go all the way.

Charles Bukowski — "Roll the Dice"

if you're going to try, go all the way.
otherwise, don't even start.
if you're going to try, go all the way.
this could mean losing girlfriends,
wives, relatives, jobs and
maybe your mind.
…

The Row quotes the doorway, not the whole house. The poem is still in copyright, and we don't pirate the poets we honor — the rest of it lives in The Flash of Lightning Behind the Mountain (2004), where it was published, and it is worth the trip.
