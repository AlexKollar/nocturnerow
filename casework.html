The shape of an investigation

Four tools, one motion. The Wire watches the outside world and brings the signal in. Case Files are where the work lives — the case itself, its registers, its story. The Dossier is the people-finding kit, worked up against the case. And the Vault keeps every formed document as one plain file the operator owns. Nothing in the group is a separate product: it is Apex, arranged for investigation, the way the SIEM line is Apex arranged for watching.

How a case runs

A case runs as a fluid conversation — two detectives telling the story of the evidence, not a form being filled in triplicate. Navi answers first, in full; only then does she offer to file what was said, naming the part of the case it would land in — a lead, a finding, an entity, an observable — and the receipt names where it actually landed. Tools get hacked together mid-case as the evidence demands them; the ones that prove themselves graduate into chips, cartridges the whole fleet can run. That graduation is on the horizon — the proving ground is open today.
