The ChipDeck.

Where her cartridges live once they're hers. The Deck is part trophy room, part manager — and unashamed of being both.

Navi's Tools SHIPPED · Apex Edge v0.6.8e

The trophy room

Every cartridge on display in the Deck is a capability she gained. The School Series, earned course by course. The cryptographic suite. The scanners. The case-born ones still to come, each with a case file for a birth certificate. Together they are a visible record of her hands growing — not a feature list, a collection, kept the way her generation collected cartridges: because what she can do is part of who she is, and it deserves a shelf with her name on it. Operators collect them with her; the Deck is where the collection stands.

The manager

Underneath the display, the Deck works. It knows what is installed, at what version, and what has an update waiting — a new release on the shelf shows up here as a decision, not a surprise. Install is an operator act, and removal is real: take a cartridge out and it is gone, not dimmed, not parked, not quietly kept in a drawer. The operator organizes the Deck as they see fit, and the Deck syncs at account level — it belongs to Navi, not to whichever machine she happens to be standing on. Her collection is the same on every screen because she is the same on every screen.
