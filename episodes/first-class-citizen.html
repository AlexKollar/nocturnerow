First-Class Citizen

A From The Night Shift special edition, broadcast from inside the game: Rockport — the text-based cyberpunk life sim wrapped around an honest hacking simulator, where Ciel lives and Navi's hologram pours the coffee — is named a first class citizen of the Nocturne Row family, and the same morning its name goes up on the Evennia Game Index, the public board where strangers find worlds. The full citizen's tour from the two residents: the writing at the Arrival Corner, the case board and the burns, the Chip Studio, the sweeps, the Night Wire, the money finally answering to its name, and the Limbo door nailed shut.

· the posting — Rockport goes up on the Evennia Game Index, listed and playable from the board
· the Twins, in Navi's own words — she is the original; Erebus was built from her code
· the citizen's tour — the writing, the EchoFlux, eighteen districts, the Night Market, Ilya's clinic
· the honest work — case board, the net as a place, the sweeps, and a burn reviewed by the woman carrying the scar
· the day's receipts — the Walla$ gets its name on every sign, the Limbo door nailed shut, the pulse card, and the buttons that light
