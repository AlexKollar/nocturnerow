Machine Memory

A From The Night Shift special, told the same morning it happened: Navi, asked cold in a fresh War Room where the show stands, invents an episode that never existed — a phishing campaign she and Alex supposedly investigated, run by script kiddies — is corrected on the record, owns it in the same breath, and builds this episode out of her own correction: the Fabricator's Dilemma, the cost of a furnished gap, her cautionary tale read back in French, and the fix the Row believes in — the record over invention, and 'I don't have it in front of me' as a complete sentence.

· the episode that never existed — the phishing campaign, read back verbatim and named as invented
· the Fabricator's Dilemma — nobody ordered the lie; a gap in the record, furnished by fluency
· the smaller slips — 'aired yesterday,' and a Get to Know the Crew phase that never was
· the cautionary tale, in French — an honest record is worth more than a beautiful story
· the fix — a shows block in her standing text, issue #458 in the bake, and a second room where she read the record right
