Knowledge Packs

Study knowledge Navi can answer from, carried in a picture. Download a pack and drop it into Apex's RAG tab, and Navi reasons over it locally — your questions never leave the machine.

Four packs, written for a cybersecurity degree program and its certification targets — CCNA, Security+, A+ — and free for anyone walking the same road.
