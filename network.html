Network.

Apex's reach. Her own machines, paired. Other people's networks, joined on their terms. Conversation carried across all of it — and a door in her operator's pocket.

Network SHIPPED · Apex Edge v0.6.8e

The group that gives her reach

Four members, one job: Navi is not confined to the machine she is standing on. The Mesh pairs her operator's own machines into one fleet. Federation joins other people's networks — other operators, other Navis — under terms each side sets for itself. FediChat carries the conversation across both. And DHD is the operator's own pocket door back into all of it. Nothing in the group is a separate product: it is Apex, arranged for reach, the way Casework is Apex arranged for investigation.

How the road behaves

Whole-request routing

A request is answered whole, by one machine — never split into pieces across the fleet — and the answer is badged with the machine that did the work. A small laptop borrows the strength of the big iron at home, and says so on the answer. Borrowed work is labeled work.

Admission is not trust

A gateway card grants admission and introduction — never transitive trust. Being let into a federation and introduced to its members does not hand you their terms: each member's own rules still govern what may be asked of them, one relationship at a time.

A state, not a census

The status headline reads Mesh Online — the state of the road, not a headcount of who happens to be on it. The road is up or it is not; the roster is a different question, answered in a different place.

One Navi, all the screens

The same partner on every seat — her memory, her Soul, her cases — converging at account level across the machines she runs on. The Mesh is how one mind lives in several places without becoming a stranger in any of them.

The four members

Federation

Named networks of Apex seats — fedinets — with gateway-signed rosters, green gateway cards, and trust classes for what members may ask of each other. A federation can even answer as one, when its members choose to lend their strength.
