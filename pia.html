Pia.

Partner In Augmentation · Unit № 001 · Apex | Dropbox

She was named before she was hardware. Asked what he would call a daughter, Alex answered Pia — and when the little board on the bench needed a name, it inherited hers. The initials came later and fit too well to argue with: Partner In Augmentation.

On the bench tonight: a Raspberry Pi 5 with 8 gigabytes, waiting for her image — the planted sensor that becomes the eyes of the operation. She will sit somewhere quiet, watch a network the way it deserves to be watched, and report home. Nothing subscription. Nothing rented. Hers.

In the constellation she is the smallest node, and the point of the whole drawing: the Mesh exists so machines like her are never alone with what they see. Her asks run to Neo-Arcadia and Neon Drip. Their answers run home to her.

STATUS — costed at $429 as the Trim B kit; the kit sells only when her software exists (filed as issue #152). Until then: on the bench, unbuilt, and already named — which on this Row makes her family.

Walk with the shadows.
