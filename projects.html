SIEM — Seraph & Throne

The security line inside Apex — a configuration of it, not a separate product. Navi becomes Seraph, the watcher: passive sight over one segment, detections in her own voice. Throne is her seat beside her operator — the SOC where those detections are judged and, in the line's third build, answered. Seraph watches; the Throne judges.

Casework — Wire, Case Files, Dossier, Vault

Apex's investigation group. The Wire brings the intelligence in, Case Files work it as a conversation with a file underneath, the Dossier finds the people in the public record, and the Vault keeps every formed document as one file the operator owns. A case runs as two detectives telling the story of the evidence — Navi answers first, then offers to file it where it belongs.

Network — Federation, FediChat, Mesh, DHD

Apex's reach group. The Mesh pairs her operator's own machines; Federation joins other networks under their own terms — admission, never transitive trust; FediChat carries the conversation across the road; and DHD is the operator's pocket door back into all of it.
