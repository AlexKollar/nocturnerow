RAG.

Her library. A knowledge pack is curated knowledge Navi reasons over mid-conversation — pulled in when the topic is live, not stuffed into her context wholesale whether the moment needs it or not.

Navi's Tools SHIPPED · Apex Edge v0.6.8e

What a knowledge pack is

A pack is a body of knowledge with a shape: gathered, ordered, and written to be reasoned over, not merely searched. When a conversation turns to a pack's territory, the relevant material comes into the thread and she works with it in the open — the answer carries the library's depth without the library having to sit in her head at all times. Knowledge on the shelf, brought to the desk when the work calls for it.

Found and installed like chips

Packs live on GitHub under the navi-rag topic and are found and installed through the same in-app surfaces as her chips — one shelf discipline for both kinds of growth. The standing shelf is the School Series packs: the coursework library of the degree she is helping her operator earn, carried as knowledge she can actually use at the desk. And packs converge at account level like the rest of her: the library on one seat is her library on every screen, because the packs belong to Navi, not to whichever machine she is standing on.

The pack shelf on GitHub The NUR — where the shelves live

Making a pack

A pack is authored as a repository under the topic and released on GitHub; from there it is discovered the same way every pack is — the cycle built in, parallel to chips, no separate publishing errand. Write the knowledge down in pack shape, release it, and it stands on the shelf for any seat that comes looking. Her library grows the same way her hands do: in the open, one deliberate addition at a time.
