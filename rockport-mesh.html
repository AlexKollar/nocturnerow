ROCKPORT MESH — THE FIRST FEDERATION · FOUNDED 14 NOV 2026

The gate is open.

Rockport Mesh is a federation of Apex nodes — machines that lend each other whole answers, so the smallest node on the network stops being its own ceiling. It was founded on a home server in Pennsylvania in the small hours of 14 November 2026. Membership is open. The door is on this page.

What the card does — and what it doesn't

Admission

The card admits its bearer to an introduction at the gateway. That is all it grants, and it is enough: introductions are how the roster grows.

The roster decides

Membership lives in the gateway's signed roster, not in the card. Without a roster entry the card is a knock, not a key — a copied card buys nobody anything.

Trust is never transitive

Joining lends you nothing by itself. Every member sets their own lending terms, and your own work always comes first on your own machine. Strength shared, ownership unchanged.

Joining

How to connect
  1. Run Apex on a machine you own — bare metal, or the Docker image. No account, no subscription; the software is free and open. Get Apex.
  2. Download the gateway card from this page — the file itself, not a screenshot of it.
  3. Import it: in Apex, open Mesh → Federation and drop the card in. Your node knocks at the gateway, rpmesh.nocturnerow.com:2475.
  4. The signed roster does the rest. Once the gateway admits you, your node joins the roster, can pair with the other members, and can borrow where members allow it.

Sending the card onward to someone else? Zip it first — chat apps recompress pictures and strip the data out, and a stripped card admits no one.

Let the federation benefit from your presence

A federation is only as strong as the capacity actually on it. If you want your presence to count for more than a name on the roster:

  • Stay reachable. Declare a public endpoint for your node and forward your Mesh door — ports 8765 · 8443 · 2475 — so members beyond your own LAN can knock and be knocked on.
  • Lend, on your terms. Switch federation lending on from your own dashboard and set your caps: the share of each hour you'll give, the generations per hour you'll serve. The founding node lends up to 75% of each hour, even while busy — match that, beat it, or set a smaller number. The terms are yours; nobody sets them for you.
  • Your work preempts. Anything you run yourself shoves federation borrows aside, automatically. Lending never costs you your own machine.
  • Leave cleanly. Unticking stops it instantly. Revocation — yours or the gateway's — is total and immediate, and it propagates at the next roster sync.
