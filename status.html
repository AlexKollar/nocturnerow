How to read this board

Operational

The check was performed and the door positively answered — a page served, a handshake completed, a banner spoken. Nothing on this board is ever assumed up.

Down

The check was performed and the door failed it: refused the connection, answered with an error, or gave no answer at all inside the timeout. A down tick names what the watcher actually saw.

Unknown

The watcher could not perform the check — its own route was blocked or intercepted. Unknown is the board refusing to guess: it says blind here, not dark there.

Watched from outside

The probes run off the house network, on purpose. A monitor living on the server cannot report the server's death — tonight's outage would have taken the witness with it. This one files from the street.
