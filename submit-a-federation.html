SUBMIT A FEDERATION — FOR LISTING IN THE DIRECTORY

Fly your own flag.

A federation you founded can be listed in the Directory, next to Rockport Mesh — its name, its gateway, and a door to its gate. It starts here, with the card itself: this page reads your gateway card in your own browser, shows you the listing it would print, and hands you a submission with every field already filled from the card. Nothing typed, nothing to typo.

Step 1 — Let the card speak

Drop your gateway card (.png) here — or click to choose the file.

The file itself, not a screenshot of it. Your browser reads it locally; nothing is uploaded by this page.

What happens next

The button opens a GitHub issue with everything prefilled from your card. Drag the same .png into that issue — uploading the card to GitHub is the submission; the issue is the knock at the Row's door.

Then the gauntlet, the same three checks for everyone: the card's data parses · its signature verifies against the gateway it names · the gateway's door answers and presents the fingerprint the card promises. A pass adds you to the Directory with the verified date stamped under your name. A failure gets a comment on your issue naming the stage that refused you — never silence, and you can fix the road and knock again. Submissions are processed in batches; expect the verdict within a day, usually much faster.

A listing is a pointer, not a promise. Your roster remains yours; the Row vouches only that your gate answered when we knocked. A listing can come down the way it went up: a gate that stays dark, a card that stops verifying, or conduct that makes the Row unwilling to point at it.

Not founded one yet?

A federation starts on your own machine: run Apex, found the federation from your dashboard, mint the gateway card. The explainer walks the distinction between the roads and the countries; Rockport Mesh's gate page is the working example.
