SUBMIT A FEDERATION — FOR LISTING ON THE ROW

Fly your own flag.

A federation you founded can be listed on the Federations page, next to Rockport Mesh — its name, its gateway, and a door to its gate. Listing is curated by the Row: a listing is a pointer, not a promise. Your roster remains yours; the Row vouches only that your gate answered when we knocked.

What to send

Open an issue on this site's repository, titled "Federation submission: <your federation's name>", with:

  • The federation's name — the flag you fly.
  • The gateway node's name and its public endpoint (host:port) — the door the world knocks on.
  • One or two sentences on what the federation is for. These run as your listing's description, lightly edited for length.
  • The gateway card, as a file. Attach the .png itself to the issue. If it has to pass through a chat app first, zip it — chat apps recompress pictures and strip the data out, and a stripped card is a dead card. This is the one rule that matters.
  • Optionally: your lending posture in a line (caps, hours, lend-while-busy or not), so visitors know the gate's terms before they knock.

Open a submission issue

How listing works

The Row imports your card and knocks at the endpoint you gave us. If the gateway answers and the roster signature verifies, the listing goes up on the Federations page. If the gate doesn't answer, the issue stays open and we tell you what we saw — you fix the road, we knock again.

A listing can come down the same way it went up: a gateway that stays dark, a card that stops verifying, or a federation whose conduct makes the Row unwilling to point at it. No drama, no appeal court — it's a page on our site, and the door is ours to keep.

Not founded one yet?

A federation starts on your own machine: run Apex, found the federation from your dashboard, mint the gateway card. The explainer walks the distinction between the roads and the countries; Rockport Mesh's gate page is the working example.
