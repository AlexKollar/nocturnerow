The Wire.

Outside-in cyber intelligence — what the world is shouting about, worked as a surface instead of scrolled as a feed. The Wire is one half of the Row's intelligence; Seraph, watching your own segment from inside, is the other.

The Wire SHIPPED · Apex Edge v0.6.8e

A working surface, not a headline wall

The Wire gathers curated threat-intelligence feeds into one surface inside Apex, arranged to be worked — searched, watched, filed from — rather than doomscrolled. A story on the Wire is raw material: it can be read, chased through the deep search, put under a watch, or born as a case. What it never is is decoration.

Deep search, through named doors

Beyond the feeds, the Wire searches a registry of doors — named, public sources with known shapes: NVD for the vulnerability record, KEV for what is known to be exploited, EPSS for the odds a flaw gets used. Every answer arrives cited to the door it came through. And a source with no door is refused by name — never scraped around, never laundered through a mirror. The refusal is on the record like everything else.

Search that forgives

Each Wire tab carries its own fuzzy search — close spellings, partial names, the half-remembered CVE from a headline at 3 a.m. The surface meets the operator's memory where it is, instead of demanding the exact string or returning silence.

The watchlist stays home

The operator keeps a watchlist — terms, names, infrastructure — and the Wire matches it against what flows past. The matching happens locally, on the operator's own seat: the list itself never leaves the machine. Only matches travel, and a match is a lead with its source attached, not a verdict.

Pulse — watches that report to cases

A Pulse watch keeps a topic under observation on a three-hour cadence, with a check at the greeting besides. Its hits do not pile up in a notification tray: they land on the reference shelf of the case the watch is attached to, cited and waiting, where the detectives working that case will find them in context.

A story can become a case

When a Wire story earns it, it gives birth to a case — opened with its first lead already cited to the story that raised it, a brief written, and the follow-up tasks staged for the operator's word. The outside world's noise becomes the case system's evidence, with the chain intact from the first step.

The Wire is the outside-in half of the Row's intelligence — the world's signal, gathered. Seraph is the inside-out half: your own segment, watched from within. Together, Navi stops guessing — she can tie what the world is shouting about to what is actually exposed, listening, or beaconing on your wire.
