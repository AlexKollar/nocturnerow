Vault.

The keeping place. Quiet on purpose — the Vault's whole argument is that your documents are files, and files are yours.

Vault SHIPPED · Apex Edge v0.6.8e

One file, owned outright

Every formed document in Apex is a single markdown file — readable in any editor, greppable from any shell, movable with any tool you already trust. There is no database underneath and no lock-in on top: walk away from Apex tomorrow and the documents walk with you, intact. The one-file doctrine is the Vault's entire design, and it does not bend.

Where case documents form

Case documents form here from the case registers — leads, findings, entities, observables, tasks, evidence, timeline — rendered as one document per case at Cases/<Type>/<Name>.md. The case system works the registers; the Vault is where the story they tell takes its final shape.

Reading first

Markdown in the Vault renders — reading-first, the document as a document, never raw markup and never a split view with the machinery showing. On the phone it is a two-state thing: the list, then the reader, each whole at a phone's width.

Across your seats

The Vault syncs on the operator's account across their seats — the same documents on the desk machine, the laptop, the phone, under the account-level sync the whole system rides. One Navi, all the screens; one Vault, all the seats.

Deletion is real

Delete a case and its formed document here is removed with it — gone means gone, and the case keeps only its custody line (see Case Files). Nothing in the Vault lingers after its reason is gone.
